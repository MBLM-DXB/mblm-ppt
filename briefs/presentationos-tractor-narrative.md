# PresentationOS — Tractor Configurator Positioning Brief
HARD RULE: Use only facts and content in this brief. Do not invent metrics, customer results, quotes, dates, or source claims. Where the brief says "request/calculate" or gives an illustration, present it as a request or illustration—not as proven tractor-customer results.

## Positioning
- Story must begin with commercial result, not presentation software / AR / 3D tech.
- Core: PresentationOS turns complex product configurations into high-converting digital sales experiences.
- If the tractor configurator converts more leads into sales than the customer's other lead sources or digital experiences, this can become the foundation of company positioning.

### Recommended headline
Turn complex products into sales experiences that convert.

### Supporting message
PresentationOS helps manufacturers present, configure, and sell complex products through interactive 3D experiences—across websites, dealerships, field sales, exhibitions, and showrooms.

### Proof line (carefully validated wording only)
A US tractor manufacturer’s 3D configurator became its highest-converting digital experience from lead to completed sale.
- Precise wording must match what the data actually proves.
- Avoid “highest-converting sales channel” unless every relevant channel was measured.
- If exact numbers are not in this brief, do NOT invent them; frame as claim to validate / case study to complete.

### Core value proposition
PresentationOS helps manufacturers increase sales conversion by making complex products easier to explore, configure, and understand in interactive 3D. The same centrally managed product experience can be deployed across websites, dealers, field sales, trade shows, and showrooms.

Four outcomes: Higher conversion; Better product understanding; Content reuse across channels; Centralized control.

### USP
A sales-focused 3D product-experience platform that connects interactive product configuration with centrally managed presentations across the complete buyer journey.

Category framing (prefer): 3D product selling; Digital product experience; Visual configuration; Immersive sales enablement.
Avoid leading with: Presentation software; Sales toolkit; AR platform; PowerPoint alternative; 3D visualization software.
Lead with: 3D product-selling platform for manufacturers.

### Elevator pitch
PresentationOS is a 3D product-selling platform for manufacturers. It lets buyers and sales representatives explore and configure complex products interactively, then deploys those experiences across websites, dealerships, field sales, exhibitions, and showrooms. One US tractor customer reports that its 3D configurator produces its strongest lead-to-sale conversion.

## Tractor case — what must be extracted (NOT filled with invented numbers)
Essential metrics to request/calculate (show as case-study data needs, not fake KPIs):
Funnel: visitors; started configuring; completed configuration; submitted lead; qualified leads; dealer quote; purchased; AOV; time lead→sale.
Compare vs: website product pages; request-a-quote forms; brochure downloads; paid-search LPs; dealer-generated leads; exhibition leads; other tools.
Commercial: revenue influenced; tractors sold; pipeline; lead-to-sale conversion; uplift vs benchmark; CPL; ROI on $30,000 annual license; payback; incremental sales to recover license.

### ROI illustration (NOT the customer’s result)
Break-even sales = annual license cost ÷ contribution margin per additional sale.
Example only: if contribution margin is $10,000 per incremental sale, a $30,000 license breaks even after three incremental sales. Label clearly as illustration.

### Recommended case-study structure
Headline: How a US tractor manufacturer turned interactive 3D configuration into its highest-converting lead-to-sale experience
Challenge: Tractors have multiple features, attachments, use cases, and configurations hard to communicate via static specs/brochures.
Solution: Interactive 3D configurator — explore, understand options, configure before sales process.
Deployment places: Public website; Dealer locations; Field-sales meetings; Exhibitions; Showrooms; Tablets/touchscreens.
Result slots (empty until data): conversion rate; comparison; sales count; revenue/pipeline; usage; customer quotation.
Expansion: more models; configs; dealer access; exhibition; regions; languages.

## ICP
Primary vertical: agricultural and heavy equipment (not industrial companies in general).
Manufacturers with: products ~$25,000+; multiple configs/attachments/options; dealer/distributor networks; expensive to transport; significant trade-show participation; buyers need visual understanding; public CAD/3D assets; existing quote-request funnel; enough web traffic to measure conversion.
Priority segments: Tractors/ag machinery; Construction; Material-handling; Commercial vehicles; Landscaping/grounds; Industrial machinery; Marine; Specialized attachments/implements.
Triggers: new range launch; major trade show; website redesign; online configuration; dealer expansion; new geography; experience center; replacing PDFs/static selectors; digital commerce/lead-gen investment.

## Commercial offer
Average annual license anchor: $30,000. Sales-assisted motion.

Purpose: Prove engagement and conversion on one product.

Commercial: $10,000–$15,000 paid pilot; credit some/all toward first annual license; define success metrics before build; no free pilot unless prospect gives equal value (public case study, significant data, major partner distribution).

Starting from $30,000 annually. Define limits: product experiences; users; traffic/usage; channels; analytics; support; hosting; updates.

### 3. Separate line items
License; 3D asset prep; experience design; configuration logic; systems integration; ongoing content updates.

### 4. Expansion
Additional models; BUs; dealer-network; trade-show mode; showrooms; languages; CRM; advanced analytics; PIM; regions/brands.

## Sales narrative
Discovery questions (use as slide content): how buyers compare models; % website leads→qualified; %→sales; best-converting digital experience; dealer routing; transport cost to exhibitions; models dealers can display; 3D/CAD available; how often specs change; highest-margin/strategic products.
