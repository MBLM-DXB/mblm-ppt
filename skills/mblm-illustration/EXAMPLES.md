# mblm-illustration — Application examples

## Brand systems

* **Metaphor:** A city of oversized type blocks and grid towers; tiny figures walk between letterform buildings.
* **Accent:** Yellow `#FFF200` or Violet `#9700DC`
* **Background:** Stark white
* **Shapes:** Typographic slabs, swatch cubes, arched portals, scaffold lines
* **Filename idea:** `illustration_brand_system_city.png`

## Digital marketing

* **Metaphor:** A funnel landscape or signal towers broadcasting to tiny walkers on a plain.
* **Accent:** Magenta `#EC008C` or Cyan `#00AEFF`
* **Background:** Paper gray or white
* **Shapes:** Funnel terraces, antenna masts, halftone clouds of dots as “reach”
* **Filename idea:** `illustration_digital_marketing_signal.png`

## Web development

* **Metaphor:** Stacked translucent slabs / browser frames as cliffs; tiny builders climb stairs of code blocks.
* **Accent:** Blue `#006AF1` or Green `#00D300`
* **Background:** Stark white
* **Shapes:** Layered rectangles, wireframe grids, ladder/stair geometry, server-stack monoliths
* **Filename idea:** `illustration_web_dev_stack.png`

## Prompt fill pattern

| Field | Brand systems | Digital marketing | Web development |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--topic` | Brand system as a city | Inbound signal landscape | Product stack as cliffs |
| `--accent` | yellow | magenta | blue |
| `--background` | white | paper | white |
| `--action` | tiny figures walk between typography blocks | tiny figures receive signals from towers | tiny builders climb layered slabs |
| `--shapes` | towers, grids, arched portals | funnel terraces, masts, halftone clouds | browser frames, wire grids, stairs |
