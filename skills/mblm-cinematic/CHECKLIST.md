# mblm-cinematic — Fit checklist & avoid list

## Fit checklist (all should pass)

- [ ] Cool blue-teal base grade present
- [ ] Exactly one warm light source readable
- [ ] Backlight / rim preferred over flat frontal key
- [ ] Shallow DOF (or intentional deep stillness with cinematic grade)
- [ ] Faces profile, soft, or obscured — not portrait-studio frontal
- [ ] Motion is either blur **or** stillness (chosen on purpose)
- [ ] Negative space available if used as cover / section / closing
- [ ] 16:9 framing suitable for deck
- [ ] Filename `photo_<slug>.png`

## Avoid list

* Smiling corporate headshots / LinkedIn portraits
* Flat frontal office fluorescent lighting
* Handshake / fist-bump stock clichés
* Busy in-frame logos or UI screenshots as the whole story
* Cartoon / vector illustration look (use `mblm-illustration` instead)
* Mixing this with monoline diagram rules (diagrams stay black/white)
* Full-bleed photos under dense body copy on content slides (use wells / half-bleed)

## Slide-role quick map

| Role | Bleed | Overlay | Crop hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover | Full | Dark for title | adaptive / hero_page |
| Section divider | Full or large | Dark / gradient bar | adaptive / hero_page |
| Closing | Full or black field | Dark for Supertext | adaptive / hero_page |
| Content | Half / well | Usually none | framed / meet |
